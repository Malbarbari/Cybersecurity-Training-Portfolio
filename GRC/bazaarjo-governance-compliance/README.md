# BazaarJo Governance & Compliance Remediation

A cybersecurity governance and compliance remediation project based on a post-breach review of the fictional e-commerce organization **BazaarJo**.

## Project Overview

The report identifies governance and compliance weaknesses that increased the likelihood or impact of a security incident and develops a prioritized remediation plan.

## Key Governance Gaps

The assessment covers:

- Lack of Segregation of Duties (SoD)
- Missing breach-notification and incident-disclosure policy
- Unclear CISO accountability
- Weak vendor security oversight
- No defined board-level risk appetite
- Insufficient PDPL and data-privacy governance
- Lack of periodic policy review
- Weak security-risk monitoring and board reporting

## Remediation Approach

The gaps are prioritized using business impact and regulatory risk, with a remediation roadmap covering:

- **Immediate:** 0–3 months
- **High:** 3–6 months
- **Medium:** 6–9 months
- **Low:** 9–12 months

The report also defines ownership, milestones, required resources, estimated cost, and KPIs for the highest-priority governance improvements.

## Top Remediation Priorities

1. Implement Segregation of Duties
2. Establish a formal breach-notification framework
3. Strengthen PDPL and vendor-security governance

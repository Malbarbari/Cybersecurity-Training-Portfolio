# BazaarJo Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

A cybersecurity risk-management case study focused on identifying, analyzing, prioritizing, treating, and monitoring risks following a security incident affecting the fictional e-commerce organization **BazaarJo**.

## Project Scope

The project covers:

- Risk context and organizational risk appetite
- Cybersecurity risk register
- Likelihood and impact analysis
- Critical risk prioritization
- Risk treatment decisions
- Risk ownership
- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Management and board reporting flow

## Key Risks Assessed

The assessment includes customer-data exposure, payment-card compromise, unauthorized source-code modification, service outage, regulatory exposure, reputational damage, vendor compromise, and weaknesses in detection and response.

## Top Priorities

The report identifies three critical priorities:

1. Customer Data Exposure
2. Unauthorized Source Code Modification
3. Regulatory & Legal Exposure

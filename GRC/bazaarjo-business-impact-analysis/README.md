# BazaarJo Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A Business Impact Analysis project for the fictional e-commerce organization **BazaarJo**, developed after a security incident and approximately two hours of service disruption.

## Project Objective

The purpose of the BIA is to identify critical business processes, assess the impact of disruption, document key dependencies, and define appropriate **Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs)** and **Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs)**.

## Processes Assessed

- Online Shopping / Order Processing
- Payment Processing
- Website & Application Operations
- Merchant Operations
- Customer Support
- Customer Account Management

## Top 3 Critical Processes

1. **Online Shopping / Order Processing** — RTO: 1 Hour, RPO: 15 Minutes
2. **Payment Processing** — RTO: 1 Hour, RPO: 5 Minutes
3. **Website & Application Operations** — RTO: 1 Hour, RPO: 15 Minutes

## Analysis Areas

- Financial impact
- Customer impact
- Regulatory / legal impact
- System dependencies
- People dependencies
- Vendor dependencies
- Recovery priorities
- RTO and RPO justification
